Jervis app privacy policy

Controller

The controller is Mazlušek s.r.o., company ID 07656980, Vršovců 2120/1a, 709 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic, e-mail info@mazlusek.com.

About the app

Jervis (com.jervis.mobile) is a personal AI assistant app for Android by Mazlušek s.r.o. The app is currently in internal testing.

Sign-in

Users sign in to the app with a Keycloak account. We process the e-mail address and name from that account.

Microphone and voice data

The app uses the microphone, including a foreground service of type microphone, while the user talks to the assistant. Voice audio and chat messages are sent to the Jervis server, where the assistant processes them.

Security in transit

Data is encrypted in transit.

Ads, sharing and purchases

The app contains no ads. We do not sell data or share it with third parties for advertising purposes. The app has no in-app purchases.

Retention and deletion

You can request deletion of your data at info@mazlusek.com.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz). Contact us at info@mazlusek.com.

Effective

Effective from 23 November 2026.
